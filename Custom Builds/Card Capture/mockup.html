Why this is easier than email

  • They never leave the text message. No email app, no attachments, no scanning.
  • One big button per screen, large readable type, and a "bigger text" button.
  • The camera opens right from the page. They take the picture and it's sent.
  • Your caller sees exactly which step they're on and what to say next.

What the real version needs

  • A texting service (such as Twilio) to send the link from your business number.
  • A small secure website: one link per client, expiring after 24–48 hours.
  • Storage for the card photos, plus a push into your client files or CRM.
  • The signature can stay in DocuSign (embedded right in step 3), or be captured here with a signed-and-timestamped audit record.

Decisions for your team

  • Keep DocuSign for the signature, or replace it?
  • Where should finished packets land: a shared drive, your CRM, email to a team inbox?
  • Should a family member be able to finish the link for them?
  • Automatic reminder text if a link isn't finished within a day?
