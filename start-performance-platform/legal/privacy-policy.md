# Privacy Policy

**Start Performance**
**Effective Date: July 3, 2026**

---

## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Start Performance ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Start Performance platform ("Platform"). 

The Platform is accessed exclusively by authorized users of subscribing business organizations ("Clients"). This policy covers our role as a data processor on behalf of those Clients, and our limited direct data collection.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account and Access Information
When your organization sets up your account, the following information is stored in the Platform:
- Your name
- Your email address (for PIN reset and notifications)
- Your role within your organization
- Access credentials (your PIN is stored in encrypted form — we cannot read it)
- Date your account was created

### 2.2 Usage Data
When you use the Platform, we may log:
- Login timestamps and session activity
- Actions taken within the Platform (records created, edited, deleted)
- Browser type and operating system (for diagnostic purposes)
- IP address

### 2.3 Business Data You Enter
The Platform stores all data you input on behalf of your organization — including contacts, companies, notes, tasks, estimates, proposals, contracts, and related records. This data belongs to your organization. We process it solely to provide the Platform's functionality.

---

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information collected to:
- Authenticate your identity and authorize access to the Platform
- Deliver and operate the features of the Platform
- Send transactional communications (PIN reset emails, system notifications)
- Diagnose and fix technical issues
- Maintain security and detect unauthorized access

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your business data for advertising.

---

## 4. How We Share Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties except:

- **Service Providers:** Hosting, email delivery, and infrastructure providers who process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations
- **Your Organization:** Your organization's administrator has access to all data associated with your account
- **Legal Requirements:** Where required by law, regulation, or valid legal process
- **Business Transfers:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with appropriate confidentiality protections

---

## 5. Data Storage and Security

- All Platform data is stored on servers hosted within [YOUR HOSTING REGION]
- We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and implement access controls to limit who can access stored data
- PINs are hashed using a secure one-way algorithm and cannot be recovered by anyone, including us
- We conduct regular backups but recommend that your organization also maintain independent backups of critical data

---

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your organization's subscription is active. Upon termination of your organization's subscription, data may be retained for up to 90 days to allow for data export, after which it will be deleted from our systems unless legal obligations require longer retention.

---

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:
- **Access:** Request a copy of personal data we hold about you
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate personal data
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your personal data (subject to your organization's data retention requirements)
- **Portability:** Request your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact your organization's administrator or reach out to us at the contact information below. Note that most data in the Platform is owned and controlled by your organization.

---

## 8. Cookies and Local Storage

The Platform uses browser cookies and local storage to:
- Maintain your authenticated session
- Remember your navigation preferences within the Platform

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

---

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Platform is intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18 years of age.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes via the Platform or by email. Continued use of the Platform after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
