# Home Comfort Keepers — Social Media & Visual Brand Playbook

## Core Brand Direction

Home Comfort Keepers should feel:

* Comfortable
* Trustworthy
* Modern
* Local
* Family-oriented
* Lifestyle-focused
* Premium but approachable

The brand should NOT feel:

* Overly corporate
* Cartoonish
* DIY-focused
* Cluttered
* Cheap sales flyer style
* Generic HVAC advertising

The visual direction is moving toward a:

> "comfortable living" lifestyle brand
> rather than a loud contractor advertisement.

---

# Primary Marketing Philosophy

The company is not just selling:

* insulation
* ventilation
* remodeling
* air quality

The company is selling:

* comfort
* better living
* healthier homes
* gathering spaces
* energy efficiency
* quality family life

Posts should often communicate:

> "This is how your home should feel."

NOT:

> "Buy this product now."

We sell comfort and better living — sometimes through beautiful lifestyle imagery, and sometimes through helpful, tasteful product education.

---

# Content Mix Goal

* **25–40%** Lifestyle / Comfort / Emotional posts (primary focus)
* **60–75%** Product education and real examples

---

# Product Showcase & Examples

When featuring products or services (insulation, solar attic fans, gutters, air quality systems, remodeling work, etc.):

* Always show the result and comfort the product creates — not just the product itself
* Prefer images that combine the product in context inside a beautiful, lived-in home
* Keep product shots clean, professional, and realistic — never cheap or overly promotional
* The comfortable home and family enjoyment must still feel like the hero
* Use real or photorealistic photography whenever possible

## Good Approaches

* Solar attic fan installed with bright, airy upstairs room and family enjoying the space
* Finished insulation work with a "noticeably cooler and quieter" living area
* Clean air filtration system in a modern home with fresh, healthy atmosphere
* Before/after remodeling — subtle, tasteful, lifestyle-focused
* Subtle product details in an otherwise lifestyle scene

## Rules

* Always tie the product back to comfort, health, energy savings, or better living
* Minimal text overlays on product images
* Avoid generic white-background product shots or hard-sell contractor style

---

# Preferred Post Types

## 1. Lifestyle Comfort Posts

Best performing direction.

Examples:

* Family enjoying remodeled kitchen
* Cozy living room scenes
* Comfortable upstairs bedroom
* Relaxed family gatherings
* Real life happening naturally inside remodeled spaces

Visual rules:

* candid feeling
* not everyone facing camera
* natural lighting
* believable environments
* subtle branding
* minimal overlay text

Preferred overlay examples:

* "Where Families Gather."
* "Home Is Where Life Happens."
* "Comfort Starts At Home."
* "Built For Everyday Living."

---

## 2. Product Spotlight Posts

Simple product-centered graphics perform well.

Especially:

* Solar attic fans
* Insulation
* Air filtration

Rules:

* Use REAL product photos when possible
* Minimal text overlays
* Strong contrast
* Clean composition
* One clear message only

Good examples:

* "Let It Breathe."
* "Cooler Starts Here."
* "Beat The Heat."
* "Cleaner Air."

Avoid clutter.

---

## 3. Review/Testimonial Posts

Use real customer language.

Best style:

* Large quote headline
* Realistic home/service imagery
* Minimal promotional clutter
* No fake/generated logos
* Keep focus on trust

Avoid:

* giant fake branding
* confusing technical visuals
* too much text

---

## 4. Seasonal/Fun Engagement Posts

Examples:

* Hump Day
* Friday posts
* Holiday posts
* Family Day

These should feel:

* warm
* light
* human
* relatable

NOT childish.

The mascot should be used carefully.

---

# Mascot Guidance (HOMEY)

The mascot is a HOUSE shape.

Important:

* DO NOT turn mascot into furry sports mascot
* DO NOT over-cartoon the brand
* DO NOT make mascot the entire focus constantly

Mascot should feel:

* simple
* clean
* recognizable
* subtle

Better uses:

* occasional fun post
* subtle side character
* relaxed lifestyle scenes

Avoid:

* giant logos
* costume energy
* over-branded scenes

---

# Text Overlay Rules

Less text is better.

Preferred:

* 2–6 words
* one emotional idea
* strong readability

Good examples:

* "Where Families Gather."
* "Let It Breathe."
* "Halfway There."
* "Cleaner Air."
* "Cooler Starts Here."

Avoid:

* paragraphs on images
* too many bullet points
* excessive badges/stickers
* coupon flyer layouts

---

# Color Direction

Use Home Comfort Keepers colors:

* Purple
* Orange
* White

Best usage:

* white primary text
* orange accent words/underline
* purple supporting accents

Avoid:

* giant blocks of color
* oversaturated graphics
* too many gradients

Goal:

> modern comfortable home brand
> not
> discount HVAC postcard

---

# Typography Style

Preferred:

* clean bold sans-serif
* occasional handwritten accent
* readable at thumbnail size

Avoid:

* too many font styles
* tiny text
* over-designed layouts

---

# Remodeling Content Direction

Important insight:
DO NOT show families doing DIY remodeling.

That weakens professional positioning.

Instead:
Show families ENJOYING remodeled spaces.

Examples:

* family in remodeled kitchen
* parents cooking together
* kids doing homework at island
* relaxed movie night
* open concept gathering spaces

The remodel is the environment.
Not the activity.

---

# Kitchen Remodeling Messaging

Kitchen remodeling posts should emphasize:

* gathering
* functionality
* everyday living
* comfort
* modern updates

NOT:

* demolition
* tools
* construction mess

Strong messaging:

> "A kitchen should work for your life — not against it."

---

# Attic Ventilation & Insulation Messaging

Key emotional angle:

* hot upstairs
* uncomfortable rooms
* trapped heat
* rising energy bills

Best positioning:

* airflow
* comfort
* efficiency
* natural cooling

Examples:

* "Your upstairs shouldn't feel like an attic."
* "Let It Breathe."
* "Beat The Heat."

---

# Image Style Guidance

Preferred image style:

* realistic photography feel
* candid
* warm lighting
* shallow depth of field
* subtle luxury
* clean composition
* believable environments

Avoid:

* obvious AI faces
* everyone staring at camera
* fake smiles
* over-rendered lighting
* cluttered infographic layouts
* excessive text

---

# Caption Style

Preferred captions:

* conversational
* concise
* emotionally grounded
* practical

Tone:

* warm
* professional
* comfortable
* trustworthy

Avoid:

* hype language
* over-salesy tone
* excessive exclamation points
* emoji overload

---

# Hashtag Strategy

Use focused local + category hashtags.

Examples:

* #HomeComfort
* #QuadCities
* #HomeImprovement
* #KitchenRemodel
* #EnergyEfficiency
* #AtticVentilation
* #IndoorAirQuality
* #HomeComfortKeepers

---

# Overall Brand Goal

The long-term visual identity should feel closer to:

* modern home lifestyle brand
* comfortable living brand
* premium local remodel company

NOT:

* generic contractor ads
* coupon mailers
* loud HVAC promotions

The strongest content direction discovered during this process:

> Realistic family/lifestyle imagery inside beautiful, livable spaces with minimal but emotionally resonant messaging.
