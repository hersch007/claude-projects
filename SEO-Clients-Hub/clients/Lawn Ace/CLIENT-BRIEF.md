# CLIENT: Lawn Ace

**Website:** https://lawnace.com
**Niche:** Residential lawn care & pest control company serving the CSRA (Central Savannah River Area) — homeowners in Augusta GA and surrounding suburbs.
**Services:** Lawn Fertilization, Weed Control, Mosquito Control, Tree & Shrub Care, Core Aeration, Fire Ant Control, Insect Control, Grub Control, packaged plans (Grow Plan, Pro Plan, Mosquito Plan)
**Goals:** Rank for local "lawn care near me" / city + service searches, win business away from national lawn-care franchises, drive same-day quote requests
**Platform:** WordPress (Site Kit by Google, GeneratePress-style theme)
**Location:** Augusta, GA (1048 Franke Industrial Dr, Augusta, GA 30909); service area also covers Grovetown, Evans, Martinez, Hephzibah, Harlem (GA) and North Augusta, Belvedere, Aiken, Beech Island, Graniteville (SC)

Key pages to analyze:
- Homepage (/)
- Service pages (/fertilization/, /weed-control/, /mosquito-control/, /tree-and-shrub-care/, /aeration/, /fire-ant-control/, /insect-control/, /grub-control/)
- Location pages (/augusta-ga/, plus Harlem GA and Grovetown GA per sitemap)
- /about-us/, /our-packages/, /lawn-care-tips/ (blog), /gallery/, /contact/

Notes: Strong bones — 39-URL sitemap, dedicated city location pages, deep service page content (~2,200 words on fertilization), clean robots.txt, and a genuinely strong trust signal (1,700+ 5-star Google reviews, 4.9 rating) prominently displayed on-page. Biggest gaps are E-E-A-T (no team bios, no license/certification disclosure, no blog author bylines or post dates) and structured data (no LocalBusiness/AggregateRating schema despite the huge review count — a real missed rich-snippet opportunity). 58% of homepage images are missing alt text. Meta description is exactly 160 characters and visibly cuts off mid-sentence ("Over 1,700…"), which reads as an SEO write-up left unfinished.
