Quick Wins & Recommendations
Medium effort Critical impact
Add schema to disambiguate from joewelchphotography.com
A separate, unrelated photographer operates at a near-identical domain. LocalBusiness/Person schema naming the specific specialty and service area helps Google — and clients — tell the two apart.
Low effort High impact
301-redirect the duplicate real-estate-photography.html
Two live URLs share the identical title and target keyword. Redirect the legacy .html version into /residential-real-estate-photography/ and set a canonical tag.
Low effort Medium impact
Rename & redirect hdr-photography.html
This URL's slug says "HDR" but the page is actually about vacation rental photography. Move it to /vacation-rental-photography/ with a 301 from the old slug.
Low effort Medium impact
Standardize the title-tag formula site-wide
Nearly every title repeats a long, inconsistently abbreviated city list ("Bch" vs. "Beach," "Ft. Laud" vs. "Ft. Lauderdale"). Use one formula, keep it under ~60 characters, and move full geography into the meta description.
Low effort Medium impact
Reconcile the Yelp listing's city with the site
Yelp surfaces the business under Pompano Beach, FL, while the site leads with Miami-Dade. Confirm the primary address and make every listing agree.
