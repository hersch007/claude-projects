Quick Wins & Recommendations
Low effort Critical impact
Add H1 headings to 15 product pages
15 of 19 pages have no H1 tag at all. This is a critical signal for search engines — every page needs exactly one H1 that matches what buyers would search for.
Low effort Medium impact
Rewrite meta descriptions on 5 pages
5 meta descriptions exceed 160 characters and get truncated in search results. Each should be a single compelling sentence under 155 characters.
Low effort High impact
Shorten title tags on 3 pages
3 title tags exceed 65 characters and will be cut off in Google results. Prioritize the keyword at the start of the title, then the brand name.
Medium effort High impact
Add Product schema to 15 pages
All 15 product pages lack structured data. Product schema tells Google exactly what each page sells, enabling rich results and stronger ranking signals for B2B equipment searches.
High effort High impact
Expand thin product page content
9 pages have under 150 words. Adding specifications, use cases, compatible machines, and FAQs to each product page gives Google substantive content to rank — and gives buyers a reason to stay.
High effort High impact
Start a technical blog / resource center
TecHouse has zero content marketing presence. A B2B equipment buyer researching "cold needle perforating vs. hot needle" or "in-line hole punching systems" should land on techousecorp.com, not a competitor.

What this means: A score of 71/100 reflects a site with solid technical bones — all titles present, all image alts in place, canonical tags clean — but a pervasive missing H1 issue across 15 product pages and zero schema markup. These are both fixable in a single sprint. The deeper challenge is zero organic clicks despite 700+ monthly impressions: every keyword ranking between position 14 and 82, with no clicks, means searchers are seeing TecHouse in results but not clicking. Better H1s, longer content, and Product schema will directly address this.
