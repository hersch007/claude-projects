| Broken Links | Yes | /about, /services, /contact all return 404 |

**Key Technical Issues:**

- **Missing meta descriptions on all pages:** Google will auto-generate snippets, often pulling poor-quality text. Write compelling 150–160 character descriptions for every page.
- **No schema markup:** An agency selling SEO services should be running flawless schema. The absence signals a credibility gap.
- **404s on short URLs (/about, /services, /contact):** Any backlinks or social shares using these paths are dead. Add 301 redirects immediately.
- **Only 1 blog post published:** Thin content signals to Google this is not an active, authoritative resource.
- **Unnamed founder image:** Alt text "man representing a marketing SEO expert" rather than a named professional undermines E-E-A-T.
