Quick Wins & Recommendations
Low effort High impact
Optimize title tag length on 7 pages
6 titles are under 30 characters (missing keyword opportunities). 1 titles exceed 65 characters and will be cut off in search results.
Low effort Medium impact
Fill in empty image alt text on 3 pages
Alt text is already in the code but blank. Adding keyword-relevant descriptions takes minutes and improves both accessibility and image search rankings.
Low effort Medium impact
Add canonical tags to 2 pages
Canonical tags are a single line of code. They tell Google which version of a page to index and prevent duplicate content penalties.
Low effort Medium impact
Fix multiple H1 tags on 2 pages
Each page should have exactly one H1. Multiple H1s dilute the main topic signal Google uses to understand the page.
Medium effort High impact
Add structured data (schema) to 2 pages
Schema markup helps Google display rich results (star ratings, FAQs, product details). Product and service pages especially benefit from this.
