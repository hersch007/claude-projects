## 9. Conversion & User Experience Issues

- **CTAs:** Present but generic — "Contact us today" and "Talk to a Fruth Expert" are solid but should include a value hook: "Get a Custom Quote in 24 Hours" or "Request Free Samples"
- **Contact friction:** Contact page exists; no live chat, no quote calculator, no RFQ form visible — B2B buyers expect a Request for Quote (RFQ) workflow
- **Mobile UX:** HubSpot CMS is responsive; assumed good but should be verified across product detail pages
- **Page load perception:** Not measured in this audit — run PageSpeed Insights on homepage and /products
- **Trust signals above the fold:** ISO and Verified Vendor Seal mentioned on homepage — good; could be more prominent with logo-style badges
- **Navigation clarity:** Clean top-nav (Products, Capabilities, Industries, Contact, Our Story, Learning Center) — well organized

**Key Fixes:**
- Add an RFQ/quote request form or link prominently in the header or hero section
- Standardize CTA language across all pages to "Request a Quote" or "Get Custom Pricing"

- **No content on packaging compliance/regulations** — medical, aerospace, and military buyers need to know Fruth understands their compliance requirements; articles on FDA, ISO, MIL-SPEC packaging standards would attract high-intent search traffic
- **No comparison content** — "Custom vs. Stock Bags: Which Is Right for Your Industry?" captures mid-funnel decision-making queries
- **No product material guides** — "LDPE vs. LLDPE: Which Film Is Right for Your Application?" targets specification-stage buyers who are closest to purchasing
- **No location/manufacturing origin content** — "American Made Custom Packaging" is a brand differentiator but not currently a keyword-targeted page

**Recommended Content Pieces:**
1. "How to Choose a Medical Packaging Supplier: 5 Questions to Ask" — targets medical procurement buyers, mid-funnel
2. "LDPE vs. LLDPE vs. HDPE: A Practical Guide for Industrial Packaging" — targets spec-stage buyers, high technical intent
3. "What Is Cleanroom Packaging and Why Does Your Industry Need It?" — targets awareness-stage buyers across electronics, medical, aerospace
4. "VCI Packaging Explained: How to Prevent Corrosion During Storage and Shipping" — targets industrial/automotive buyers
5. "Custom vs. Stock Bags: Cost, Lead Time, and Performance Compared" — targets any industry vertical, decision stage
6. "ISO 9001 vs. ISO 14644: What Certifications Should You Require from a Packaging Partner?" — establishes Fruth's authority and directly supports their differentiators
