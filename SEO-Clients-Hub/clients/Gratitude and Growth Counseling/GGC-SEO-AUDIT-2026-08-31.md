## 9. Conversion & User Experience Issues

- **CTAs:** Strong and specific throughout — "Schedule a Free Consultation," "Book Your 15-Minute Consult," "Start Healing with EMDR Today." Best in class for a solo therapy practice.
- **Contact friction:** Low — free consultation offer, direct booking link, phone and email visible. Well optimized.
- **Dual audience friction:** Therapy clients and clinicians (supervision, CEU, consulting) share the same navigation. A "Are you a therapist or client?" intake split would improve conversion for both.
- **Mobile UX:** WordPress/responsive assumed; verify on iOS and Android
- **Trust signals above the fold:** Testimonials, directory badges, insurance logos, and credentials are present — strong for a therapy site
- **Pricing visibility:** FAQ page shows $75–$2,600 range — transparent pricing is a positive trust signal and reduces inquiry friction

**Key Fixes:**
- Add a clinician vs. client split in the navigation or hero to reduce confusion
- Ensure insurance logos are visible on homepage above the fold (major conversion driver for therapy clients)
