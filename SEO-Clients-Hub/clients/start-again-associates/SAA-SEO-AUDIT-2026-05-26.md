**Client:** Start Again Associates

**Auditor:** Richard (SEO Consultant)

---

## 1. Overall SEO Health Score: 31 / 100

**Summary:** Start Again Associates has an exceptional product and strong credentialing, but is essentially invisible online — 1 organic visitor per month, only 2 ranking keywords, a blank homepage, and 61 documented SEO issues across 31 pages. The foundation is technically sound (SSL, sitemap, desktop speed) but the content, metadata, and mobile experience need a near-complete rebuild.

> Note: Ubersuggest scores the site at 67/100 for on-page technical checks alone. The 31/100 above reflects real-world visibility, content depth, mobile performance, and conversion readiness.

## 7. On-Page SEO & Content Analysis

**Homepage:**
- Title: Unknown / possibly missing or generic
- Meta Description: Unknown / likely missing
- H1: Likely missing or generic (page is visually blank)
- Notes: **This is the most critical single issue on the site.** The homepage has no visible content — no hero copy, no service summary, no value proposition. Google has nothing to index. First-time visitors see nothing and likely bounce immediately. A blank homepage in a YMYL (Your Money, Your Life) niche will receive zero trust signals from Google's quality raters.

**About Page:**
- Content: Good — describes 30-year history, team credentials, mission, MET(T)A Protocol
- Notes: Strong E-E-A-T content exists here but is buried. Key facts from this page belong on the homepage. Dr. Dansiger's credentials and the MET(T)A Protocol should be surfaced more prominently.

**EMDR Training Page:**
- Content: Comprehensive — covers Part I/II curriculum, consultation requirements, eligibility, fees
- Notes: This is the best-developed page on the site but is doing all the heavy lifting. Splitting Part I and Part II into separate pages would allow each to rank independently and reduce the length/complexity of the current page.

**Staff Page:**
- Content: Detailed bios for Dr. Dansiger and Roshni Chabra
- Notes: Strong E-E-A-T signals present. Add schema markup here. Add license numbers, EMDR-IA approval status, and links to professional profiles/publications.

**Resources Page:**
- Content: Past webinar thumbnails only — thin
- Notes: This should become a full content hub with articles, guides, and downloadable resources targeting search queries from clinicians.

**Contact Page:**
- Not reviewed in screenshots
- Notes: Ensure this page is indexed, has a clear form, and includes NAP information (Name, Address, Phone) for local/branded searches.

**Content Quality Summary:**
- Word count: Majority of pages are thin (12/31 pages flagged as low word count)
- Keyword targeting: Effectively absent — no intentional keyword strategy detectable
- Internal linking: Weak — navigation-only, no contextual links between pages
- CTAs: Weak — no clear "Register Now," "Book a Training," or "Get Started" CTAs visible on most pages

## 8. E-E-A-T Analysis

- [x] Founder story with 30-year history (About page)
- [x] Proprietary protocol with named creator (MET(T)A Protocol — Dr. Dansiger)
- [x] EMDR-IA Approved program status mentioned (EMDR Training page)
- [x] Published books referenced (Dr. Dansiger is a multi-book author)
- [ ] License numbers listed — NOT present
- [ ] Professional certifications (EMDR-IA, LMFT, PsyD) listed consistently — PARTIAL
- [ ] Client/trainee testimonials or reviews — NOT visible
- [ ] Press mentions or media coverage — NOT visible
- [ ] Awards or recognition — NOT visible

**E-E-A-T Gaps (High Priority for YMYL Niche):**
- **No testimonials anywhere on the site.** For an EMDR training provider, trainee quotes with name and credentials are powerful trust signals. Add to homepage and EMDR Training page.
- **License numbers and certifications not prominently displayed.** Add credentials (PsyD, LMFT, MFT, EMDR-IA Approved status) to all bios and the footer.
- **No media/press page.** Dr. Dansiger has books, podcasts, and speaking engagements — these belong on the site as authority signals.
- **No external validation links.** Link to EMDR International Association approval, book listings, podcast appearances.

**Local SEO:**
- Google Business Profile: Not claimed — missing a high-value zero-effort asset
- NAP Consistency: Unknown — no address/phone visible on screenshots
- Geographic targeting: Weak — site does not specify cities/states for in-person trainings
- Local citations: Not assessed

## 9. Conversion & User Experience Issues

- **Homepage CTA:** None — page is blank. Users who land on the homepage have no path forward.
- **Contact friction:** Contact page exists but no phone number, chat, or urgency visible in screenshots
- **Trust above the fold:** None on homepage; strong on About and Staff pages but these are secondary entry points
- **Mobile UX:** Poor load time (7.62s) will cause significant bounce rates from mobile search traffic
- **Navigation:** Flat and functional — HOME, ABOUT, EMDR TRAINING, STAFF, CONTACT, RESOURCES — but missing a direct "Book a Training" or "Register" CTA in the nav bar
- **Social proof:** Absent sitewide — no reviews, no trainee count ("1,000+ clinicians trained"), no logos

**Key Fixes:**
- Add a sticky "Register for EMDR Training" button to the navigation
- Add a testimonials section to the homepage and EMDR Training page
- Add a trust bar (e.g., "EMDR-IA Approved | 30 Years of Practice | 1,000+ Clinicians Trained") above the fold on the homepage

## 10. Content & Keyword Strategy

**Primary Audience:** Licensed clinicians (MFTs, LCSWs, Psychologists) seeking EMDR certification
**Secondary Audience:** Treatment centers and group practices seeking agency-level training
**Tertiary Audience:** Individuals seeking trauma-focused therapy

**Target Keyword Opportunities:**

| Keyword | Intent | Est. Difficulty | Priority Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| EMDR training for therapists | Commercial | Medium | /emdr-training |
| EMDR certification program | Commercial | Medium | /emdr-training |
| EMDR therapy training online | Commercial | Medium | /emdr-training |
| EMDR Part 1 training | Commercial | Low | /emdr-training-part-1 (new page) |
| EMDR Part 2 training | Commercial | Low | /emdr-training-part-2 (new page) |
| MET(T)A Protocol training | Branded | Very Low | /metia-protocol (new page) |
| mindfulness EMDR therapy | Informational | Low | Blog post |
| EMDR for addiction treatment | Informational | Low | Blog post |
| trauma-focused therapy training | Commercial | Medium | /emdr-training |
| EMDR training for addiction clinicians | Commercial | Low | /emdr-training |
| EMDR consultation hours | Commercial | Low | /consultation (new page) |
| mindfulness-based trauma therapy | Informational | Low | Blog post |

**Content Gap Analysis:**
- **No blog or articles** — clinicians searching for EMDR education land on competitors with rich content libraries
- **No dedicated consultation page** — consultation is buried inside the EMDR Training page; it deserves its own page and keyword targeting
- **No "For Agencies" or "For Treatment Centers" page** — B2B audience has no dedicated entry point
- **No MET(T)A Protocol page** — this proprietary method is a massive branded keyword opportunity currently unowned by anyone
- **No FAQ page** (or very thin) — clinicians have many questions about EMDR certification requirements; an FAQ targets long-tail searches

**Recommended Content Pieces:**
1. "What Is the MET(T)A Protocol? A Mindfulness-EMDR Approach to Trauma" — targets branded + informational queries, builds authority
2. "How to Become EMDR Certified: A Step-by-Step Guide for Therapists" — high-value informational piece, drives top-of-funnel traffic
3. "EMDR for Addiction Treatment: What Clinicians Need to Know" — targets a specific niche audience aligned with SAA's specialty
4. "EMDR Training vs. EMDR Certification: What's the Difference?" — FAQ-style content targeting comparison searches
5. "Mindfulness-Based Approaches to Complex PTSD: A Clinical Overview" — thought leadership, builds E-E-A-T
