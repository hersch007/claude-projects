YMYL Warning — E-E-A-T Is Non-Negotiable Here
This is a "Your Money or Your Life" website
Google classifies grief counseling and mental health therapy sites under YMYL — content that could significantly impact a person's health, safety, or wellbeing. For YMYL sites, Google explicitly states it holds content to the highest standards of expertise, authoritativeness, and trustworthiness. A generic small-business SEO fix won't move this site — the E-E-A-T work (credentials, licenses, professional associations, original content) is the SEO work.
Therapist profile pages are returning 404
The therapist profiles are the most important pages on this entire site from Google's perspective. They are where Dr. Liz Anderson's doctorate and Amy Fowler's credentials should live — the license numbers, specializations, and training that tell Google this is a legitimate, expert therapy practice. These pages must be rebuilt before any other SEO investment will reach its potential.

  "telephone": "+1-828-357-7388",
