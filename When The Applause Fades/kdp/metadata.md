## Description (Blurb for Amazon)

Amazon supports basic HTML. Use `<b>`, `<em>`, `<br>`, `<p>` tags.

```html
<p><b>Some love stories don't chase the spotlight. They wait for you in the quiet.</b></p>

<p>Elliot Brooks has spent five years learning how to be alone well. Widowed, a church elder, a father whose daughter is finally finding her footing — his life in Oceanside, North Carolina runs on quiet rhythms: coffee, Scripture, a spice rack alphabetized except for the paprika. He does not expect any of that to change when a global pop star knocks on his door for a two-hour photo shoot.</p>

<p>Nova Hart has spent years being seen by everyone and known by no one. Sixty thousand people scream her name every night; she goes back to a hotel suite that smells like other people's idea of luxury. She doesn't plan to stay for pie eaten straight from the tin. She doesn't plan to tell a stranger something true. But she does.</p>

<p>What begins as an afternoon becomes a friendship, and what begins as a friendship becomes something neither of them asked for and both of them want to keep — until a driveway photo turns Elliot's address into a hashtag, and the private thing they've built has to survive being public.</p>

<p>Elliot has to decide what he's willing to lose. Nova has to decide whether she can stop protecting people by disappearing. And both of them have to decide whether an ordinary, unhidden love is worth the noise it costs.</p>
```
