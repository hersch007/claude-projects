## Welcome Sequence Overview

1. **Email 1 (immediate):** Deliver reader magnet + warm welcome
2. **Email 2 (Day 3):** Your story — who you are, why you write romance
3. **Email 3 (Day 7):** About the book — sneak peek, what's coming

## List Building Strategy

- Reader magnet (free short story, bonus chapter, playlist, etc.)
- Social media CTAs
- BookFunnel group promotions
- Author newsletter swaps (once list is established)
- Back matter of published books
