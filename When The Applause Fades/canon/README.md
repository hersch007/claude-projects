# Canon

The series bible. Everything locked and true about this story lives here.

Once something is canon, it does not change without a deliberate decision and an update to this folder.

## Files to Maintain

- `series-bible.md` — world rules, setting details, recurring facts
- `timeline-canon.md` — official locked timeline (mirrors the timeline folder)
- `character-canon.md` — locked character facts (appearance, backstory, non-negotiables)
- `continuity-log.md` — running log of facts established chapter by chapter

## What Belongs Here

- Character ages, birthdays, physical descriptions
- Place names and descriptions
- Relationship histories
- Career/industry facts as they exist in the story's world
- Any detail referenced more than once

## What Does NOT Belong Here

- Working notes or "maybes" — those go in `research/` or `manuscript/`
- Marketing copy — that goes in `marketing/`

## Continuity Check Process

After each chapter is approved:
1. Note any new facts established in `continuity-log.md`
2. Check against existing canon for conflicts
3. Update character or series-bible files if new facts are now locked
