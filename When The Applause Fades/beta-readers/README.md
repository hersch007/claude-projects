## Ideal Beta Reader Mix

For a contemporary romance, aim for:
- 2–3 readers who are avid genre readers (your target audience)
- 1 reader outside the genre (for plot/logic clarity)
- 1 sensitivity reader for any marginalized identities/experiences in the text (this may be a paid professional)

## What to Send Beta Readers

1. The manuscript (Word doc or Google Doc — their preference)
2. `reader-guidelines.md` with your specific questions
3. A deadline (typically 3–6 weeks)
4. A thank-you note and your expectations

## Confidentiality

Ask beta readers to keep the manuscript confidential and not share it or quote from it publicly before publication.
