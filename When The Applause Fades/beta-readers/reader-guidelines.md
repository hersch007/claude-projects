## What I Need From You

Please read as a reader first, not an editor. Note where you:
- Got pulled out of the story
- Were confused about something
- Wanted to stop reading (and why)
- Were completely hooked

---

## Specific Questions

Please answer these after you've finished reading:

### Big Picture

1. Did the overall romance arc feel satisfying? Did you believe in the relationship?
2. Was the pacing engaging throughout, or were there stretches where your interest dipped? (Note the chapters if so.)
3. Did the ending feel earned? Did you believe the resolution?
4. Were there any plot holes or logic gaps that pulled you out of the story?

### Characters

5. Did you connect with [Protagonist]? Did you root for her?
6. Did you find [Love Interest] believable and compelling?
7. Were there any secondary characters who felt underdeveloped or unnecessary?

### Craft

8. Was the dialogue natural and distinct for each character?
9. Were there any scenes that felt too long, or that you skimmed?
10. Were there any scenes you wished were longer or explored more?

### Emotional

11. Which scene hit you hardest emotionally?
12. Was there a moment where you felt the emotional stakes weren't landing?
13. How did you feel at the end?

### Open Feedback

14. What was your favorite thing about the book?
15. What was your biggest concern or the thing that needs the most work?
16. Anything else you want me to know?

---

## How to Leave Notes

- Inline comments in the document are welcome and very helpful
- You can also write your answers to the questions above in a separate email or document
- You do NOT need to copyedit — I'm not looking for grammar fixes at this stage

## Confidentiality

Please keep the manuscript confidential. Do not share it, quote from it publicly, or post about it before publication. Thank you.
