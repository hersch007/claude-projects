## Logline

A widowed church elder in a small North Carolina beach town and a world-famous pop star fall for each other after a chance photo shoot — and have to decide whether their private, ordinary love can survive both of their public worlds finding out.

## One-paragraph synopsis

Elliot Brooks, a widowed cybersecurity CEO and church elder in Oceanside, NC, opens his door to pop star Nova Hart for what's supposed to be a two-hour promotional photo shoot. What she finds instead is pie eaten from the tin, an unhurried house, and a man who isn't performing for her. As their private friendship deepens into something neither can name, a leaked photo of Elliot's driveway turns his address into a hashtag and drags his ordinary life into her extraordinary one. Nova has to decide whether she can stop managing her exposure by disappearing; Elliot has to decide what he's willing to lose — his church leadership, his privacy, his carefully protected grief — for something he didn't ask for and can't walk away from. The book closes with both of them choosing visibility over safety: Nova naming him on national television, Elliot flying across the country to tell her the same thing in person. It ends not with a grand gesture but with two coffee mugs on a drying rack, months later, ordinary and chosen.

## What Kind of Edit We're Looking For

**Primary need: [choose one before sending — this changes both scope and cost]**
- [ ] **Line edit** — sentence-level prose, pacing, voice consistency, dialogue polish
- [ ] **Copyedit** — grammar, punctuation, consistency, house-style, light prose tightening
- [ ] **Proofread** — final-pass error catching only, post-typesetting
- [ ] **Combination line edit + copyedit**

**Not needed:** developmental editing. The manuscript has already been through an extensive structural/developmental pass — full continuity bible, chapter-by-chapter beat sheet, and a locked outline for the three structural anchor scenes (midpoint, black moment, climax). We're not looking for plot, structure, or character-arc feedback at this stage unless something breaks on read.

**Genre fit matters:** looking specifically for an editor experienced in **inspirational/Christian or faith-adjacent romance** — someone who knows CBA content conventions and pacing expectations for a faith arc running parallel to a romance arc, rather than a general-market romance editor unfamiliar with the genre's norms.
