# Launch

Launch plan, ARC distribution, promotional schedule, and launch team coordination.

## Files

- `launch-plan.md` — master timeline and task list
- `arc-tracker.md` — ARC reader list, contacts, and review status
- `promo-schedule.md` — paid and free promotional opportunities
- `launch-team.md` — launch team recruitment and coordination

## Launch Phases

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch (3+ months out)
- Finalize manuscript and cover
- Set up pre-order (if applicable)
- Begin building launch team / ARC list
- Schedule cover reveal
- Start building newsletter list

### Phase 2: ARC Phase (6–8 weeks out)
- Send ARCs to reviewers
- Begin teaser content on social
- Pitch to bloggers, bookstagrammers, BookTokers
- Schedule newsletter sequence

### Phase 3: Launch Week
- Announce across all platforms
- Push for reviews
- Respond to reader posts and tags
- Run any launch promotions

### Phase 4: Post-Launch
- Thank launch team
- Continue pushing for reviews
- Submit to BookBub and promo sites
- Plan any price promotions or box set inclusion

## Key Launch Metrics to Track

- Amazon ranking (at launch and 30/60/90 days)
- Review count (Amazon + Goodreads)
- Newsletter sign-ups
- Social media reach during launch week
- Units sold / pages read (KU)
