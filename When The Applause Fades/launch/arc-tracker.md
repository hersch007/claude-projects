## ARC Request Message Template

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm [Your Name], and I'm writing to ask if you'd be interested in reading an advance copy of my upcoming contemporary romance, *When the Applause Fades*, releasing on [DATE].
>
> [One-sentence hook.]
>
> It's [X,000] words, [heat level], and features [key tropes].
>
> If you're interested, I'd love to send you a copy via BookFunnel in exchange for an honest review posted on Amazon and/or Goodreads around launch day. No pressure if it's not your thing — I completely understand!
>
> Thanks so much for considering it.  
> [Your name]
