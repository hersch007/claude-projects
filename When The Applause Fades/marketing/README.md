# Marketing

All pitch and promotional copy for "When the Applause Fades."

## Files

- `blurbs.md` — back-cover blurb, short blurb, one-liner, tagline
- `comp-titles.md` — comparable titles with positioning notes
- `pitch.md` — query letter / pitch document
- `tropes.md` — romance tropes list for discoverability

## Blurb Hierarchy

Romance readers encounter your book at different levels of detail. You need copy for each:

| Format | Length | Used For |
|---|---|---|
| Tagline | 1 sentence, ~15 words | Social headers, ads |
| One-liner | 1–2 sentences, ~30 words | ARC request intros, quick pitches |
| Short blurb | 1 paragraph, ~75 words | Newsletters, Bookstagram captions |
| Full blurb | 2–3 paragraphs, ~200 words | Amazon, Goodreads, back cover |

## Comp Title Strategy

Comp titles should be:
- Published within the last 3–5 years
- In the same sub-genre (contemporary romance)
- Comparable in tone, NOT just theme
- Books that sold well, but not massive outliers (don't comp to Colleen Hoover unless you have a specific reason)
