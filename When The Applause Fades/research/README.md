# Research

Reference notes, industry research, and background material that informed the writing.

## What Belongs Here

- Industry research (music industry, publishing, any profession featured in the book)
- Location research (places the characters go)
- Cultural research (events, subcultures, communities represented)
- Craft research (writing technique, romance structure references)
- Interviews or conversation notes (anonymized if sensitive)
- Links to sources (with date accessed)

## What Does NOT Belong Here

- Canon facts about your story — those go in `canon/`
- Fictional world-building — that's `canon/`
- Marketing research — that's `marketing/`

## File Naming Convention

```
[topic].md
music-industry.md
tour-logistics.md
grief-research.md
```

## Source Citation Format

When saving research, note the source:

```
**Source:** [Title / URL / Person]  
**Date accessed:** [YYYY-MM-DD]  
**Key takeaway:** [1–2 sentences on what's useful for the story]
```
