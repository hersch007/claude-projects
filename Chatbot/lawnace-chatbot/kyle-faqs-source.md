## AERATION

**What it is:** Core aeration relieves compacted soil so water, air, and nutrients reach grass roots. Kyle's line: "Think of it as letting your lawn exhale."

**Why CSRA needs it:** Clay-heavy soil plus foot traffic squeezes things tight. Water runs off, fertilizer can't reach deep enough, lawn underperforms no matter how well you water.

**Best time:** Late April or early May, through summer. Warm-season grasses need active growth to recover. Don't aerate too late — lawn needs a few weeks before cold arrives.

**Frequency:** Most Augusta-area lawns: once a year. Lawns with heavy foot traffic, pets, or dense clay may benefit more often. Liquid aeration available as an ongoing add-on between core aerations.

**Plugs:** Leave them. They break down in 1-2 weeks as you mow and return nutrients. "The mess is part of the magic."

**Compaction test:** Push a screwdriver into the soil. Feels like a workout = compacted. Also: water pools/runs off, grass feels thin, high-traffic areas struggle.

## FERTILIZATION

**Timing:** Late spring after full green-up, then through summer. Grass type matters:
- Bermuda and Zoysia: more nitrogen
- Centipede and St. Augustine: lighter, more balanced

**Yellow grass:** Usually hungry, not just thirsty. "Water is the drink. Fertilizer is the meal." Sandy CSRA soil loses nutrients fast.

**Weeds:** Thick healthy grass crowds weeds out. Fertilizer + weed control together (which is what LawnAce bundles) works best.

**Frequency:** Several times a year. Spring greens it up. Summer handles heat. Fall builds strong roots for winter.

**DIY risk:** Wrong timing or wrong amount. Too much burns, too little does nothing. "The bag makes it look easy. The bag is lying."

**Results timeline:** Greener color in about 2 weeks. Thicker grass and fewer weeds build all season.

## FIRE ANTS

**Why CSRA has so many:** Warm temps most of the year, regular moisture, clay soil = luxury resort for fire ants. Active spring through early fall.

**Treatment:** One-time application **guaranteed for the entire calendar year.** If ants come back during coverage, LawnAce returns at no additional cost.

**Why DIY fails:** Store-bought products treat surface activity. Colony lives underground. Unless treatment reaches the queen, colony rebuilds or relocates. "Go after the queen, not the porch."

**Best time to treat:** Before populations peak (early spring). "Already seeing mounds? The best time is now."

**Danger:** Real risk — stings are painful, can trigger allergic reactions. Kids and pets at highest risk. Mounds hide in grass, ants swarm when disturbed. Also damages turf and creates bare spots.

## GRUBS

**What they are:** Larval stage of beetles. C-shaped, underground, eating grass roots.

**Signs:** Brown/thinning patches that don't respond to watering, grass feels spongy/pulls up easily (like a loose rug), armadillos/moles/birds digging the lawn. "If your lawn rolls back like carpet, call us. Quickly."

**Wildlife connection:** Animals digging = they smell the grubs. Close the buffet, the diggers move on.

**Treatment timing:** Before new grubs hatch and start feeding. CSRA: especially active late spring through summer. Treat too late and damage continues underground even after grubs die.

**Why they return:** Grubs mature into beetles, beetles lay eggs back in same lawn. Properly timed treatments break the cycle.

**Recovery:** Usually yes. Stop the feeding, then fertilize to help roots rebuild.

**DIY risk:** Timing is everything. Preventative products don't work on mature grubs; curative ones miss the prevention window.

## INSECTS

**Local roster:**
- **Fall armyworms** — can chew Bermuda to nothing in days. Arrive late summer.
- **Chinch bugs** — hot/dry spots, St. Augustine lawns, expanding yellow-then-brown patches.
- **Two-lined spittlebugs** — Centipede specialist. White foam blobs in turf.
- **Sod webworms** — nighttime feeders, ragged brown patches.
- **Mole crickets** — underground tunnelers in sandier soils, spongy/uneven turf.
- **White grubs** — serious enough to get their own program at LawnAce.

**Key observation:** "Most do their damage out of sight or after dark." Lawn weakens before you see the culprit.

**Diagnosis:** Thinning grass, brown patches, turf that pulls up easily, surface tunnels/mounds, OR a lawn that looks stressed despite proper watering. "Insects weaken grass from below."

**Safety:** Technicians select and apply treatments correctly. Stay off treated areas until dry (couple hours).

## MOSQUITO CONTROL

**Season:** CSRA ramps up early spring, doesn't quit until fall. Program runs **March through October**.

**How it works:** Mosquitoes rest in shaded, humid spots — under shrubs, dense landscaping, pine straw, mulch beds. Treatment is applied to those zones. Eliminates on contact and creates a protective barrier.

**Frequency:** Monthly treatments throughout peak season. Heat, rain, and breeding cycles wear on any treatment — one-and-done doesn't work.

**What customers can do between treatments:** Eliminate standing water. Plant saucers, gutters, toys, buckets, tarps after rain. "Mosquitoes need only a few days and a few tablespoons of water to breed."

**Safety:** Safe for families and pets. Stay off treated areas until dry (couple hours).

**Realistic promise:** Major reduction, not zero. "Fewer bites, more backyard." (They fly — neighbor's yard matters.)

## TREE & SHRUB

**Why shrubs brown:** Insect damage, fungal disease, nutrient deficiency, or heat/drought stress. "Brown is their version of a check-engine light." Problem starts weeks before visible symptoms.

**Program includes:** Nursery-grade fertilization, insect control treatments, disease prevention (fungus), winter deep-root support, and ongoing monitoring.

**Best time to start:** "The best time was last season. The second best time is before this season's problems start."

**Local pests:** Aphids, scale, lace bugs (sap-feeding insects), plus fungal diseases that spot leaves and spread through soil.

**Preventative argument:** Replacing a mature tree or row of established shrubs costs far more than ongoing prevention. Plants take years to replace. "Keep 'fine' looking fine."

**CSRA summers:** Strong root systems absorb water better. Well-fed plants recover faster. Healthy plants less attractive to insects.

## WEED CONTROL

**Pre-emergent timing:**
- Late winter: **February to early March** before soil hits ~55°F (stops crabgrass)
- Fall: **September to October** (stops winter weeds: annual bluegrass, henbit, chickweed)
- "Miss the window and you're stuck playing defense all season."

**Safety:** Safe for families and pets when applied correctly. Stay off until dry (couple hours).

**DIY risks in CSRA:**
1. Timing — miss the pre-emergent window
2. Wrong product on wrong grass — some herbicides seriously damage Centipede or stress Bermuda in summer heat

**Results timeline:** Post-emergent shows visible wilting/yellowing in 1-2 weeks. Tough ones (nutsedge, dallisgrass) may need follow-up. Pre-emergent results are "invisible by design."

**Service call guarantee:** "If weeds pop up between scheduled visits, let us know and we'll take care of it — on us. That's part of the plan." (Grow Plan)

**How long to see full results:** Can knock out existing weeds in a couple of treatments. But CSRA climate means weed seeds are always waiting — ongoing program prevents re-infestation. "Maintaining a healthy, weed-free lawn costs less than the cycle of cleaning up a big mess."
