About Steph

Stephanie Brashear is a licensed EMDR Therapist and EMDRIA Approved Consultant based in Providence, Rhode Island — licensed in SC, ID, and OH. She has built a trusted voice in the mental health space by making complex trauma therapy genuinely accessible.

Her content focuses on EMDR, IFS, CPTSD, and religious trauma — topics deeply underserved by mainstream wellness creators, yet actively sought by therapy clients and practicing clinicians alike. Her TikTok content consistently reaches far beyond her follower count, with individual videos earning tens of thousands — sometimes over 100,000 — organic views.

She speaks to two high-trust audiences at once: survivors navigating their healing and therapists looking for clinical insight and practice tools — making her uniquely valuable to brands in the therapy and mental health practice space.
