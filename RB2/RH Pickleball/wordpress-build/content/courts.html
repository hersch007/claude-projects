Boyd Hill

Outdoor · [FILL IN: # courts] · Rock Hill, SC

One of the most convenient outdoor spots in Rock Hill - when it’s up and running, it’s great for a game close to town. The trade-off is consistency: court time can get moved or cancelled with little notice, and there aren’t many courts. Always check the schedule before you drive over.

Bleachery Fieldhouse

Indoor · 12 courts · Rock Hill, SC

The biggest indoor option around - 12 courts means you can almost always get on, rain or shine. It’s the workhorse of Rock Hill indoor play. Just go in knowing its quirks.

👍 Pros

  • 12 indoor courts - great capacity
  • Weather-proof, central location
  • Good for groups & larger sessions

👎 Honest cons

  • Uneven lighting in spots
  • Known dead spots on the floor
  • Competitive to book at peak times
