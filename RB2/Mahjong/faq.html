🀄 American Mahjong Game Rules FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about American Mahjong rules, strategy, and how this game works.

Jokers

How do Jokers work in American Mahjong?

Jokers are wild cards — they can substitute for any tile in a set of 3 or more (Pung, Kong, or Quint). Key restrictions:

  • Jokers cannot be used in pairs or single tiles.
  • Jokers cannot be passed during the Charleston.
  • Jokers can be stolen from exposed sets (see below).
  • Some hands on the card are marked "No Jokers" — those require all real tiles.
Can I steal a Joker from someone's exposed set?

Yes! On your turn (after drawing, before discarding), if an opponent has an exposed set that contains a Joker, you may replace that Joker with the real tile it represents.

For example: if the exposed set is [4B][4B][Joker] and you hold a 4B, you can place your 4B into their set and take the Joker into your own hand. You still discard a tile this turn.

What happens if I have to discard a Joker?

In standard NMJL rules, you should never be in a position where you must discard a Joker — hold them until you can use them. In most house rules, discarding a Joker ends your hand as dead (you can no longer win that hand). This game treats discarded Jokers as dead tiles.

The NMJL Card

What is the current year's NMJL card like?

The National Mah Jongg League publishes a new card every year. The 2024 card (and this game's representative hands) typically includes these categories:

  • 2024 — hands using the digits 2, 0, 2, 4
  • Consecutive Runs — sequences like 123 456 789
  • Like Numbers — same number across three suits (e.g. 1111C 1111B 1111D)
  • 3-6-9 — multiples of three
  • Singles & Pairs — seven pairs (no Jokers allowed)
  • Winds & Dragons — honor tile hands
  • Quints — five of a kind (requires Jokers)
  • Evens (2468) — even-numbered tile hands

For the official current card, visit nationalmahjonggleague.org. This game ships with representative hands in each category — they are not guaranteed to exactly match the current official card.

What does "FF" mean on the card?

FF means a pair of Flower tiles. Any two Flower or Season tiles (Plum, Orchid, Chrysanthemum, Bamboo, Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter) count as the FF pair — they don't have to match. Jokers cannot substitute for Flowers in an FF pair.

What do C, B, D mean in hand notation?

These are suit abbreviations on the card:

  • C = Craks (Characters, red tiles)
  • B = Bams (Bamboo, green tiles)
  • D = Dots (Circles, blue tiles)

A hand marked "any suit" means all tiles in that hand must be the same suit (C, B, or D — your choice). A hand marked with specific suit letters requires those exact suits.

The Charleston

Is the Charleston mandatory?

The First Charleston (Right → Across → Left) is always mandatory. All players must participate.

The Second Charleston is optional. Any player may stop it before it begins. If anyone declines, the second Charleston does not happen.

Can I pass Jokers during the Charleston?

No. Jokers cannot be passed during the Charleston. If you receive a Joker, it stays with you. Plan your passes around keeping your Jokers.

What is the "blind pass" in the Second Charleston?

During the Second Across pass, you may look at the tiles coming your way (before passing yours across) and "steal" up to 3 of them — keeping those and substituting other tiles from your hand in their place. This is purely optional.

Gameplay

Who can call a discarded tile?

Any player may call a discarded tile for:

  • Mahjong — to win
  • Kong (4), Quint (5) — to form an exposed set, then discard
  • Pung (3) — to form an exposed set, then discard

Mahjong always wins priority over other calls. If two players want to call Pung/Kong at the same time, the player whose turn comes next gets priority (play is counter-clockwise, so the player to the left of the discarder has first priority).

What happens when I expose tiles (call a set)?

When you call a Pung, Kong, or Quint from a discard:

  1. Place the claimed tile plus your matching hand tiles face-up on the table (exposed).
  2. Discard one tile from your remaining hand.
  3. Exposed tiles cannot be taken back into your hand.
  4. Your remaining hand must still complete a valid NMJL hand pattern to win.
What happens if the wall runs out?

If the wall is exhausted with no winner, the hand is a draw. No one pays. The deal passes to the next East player and a new hand begins.

When is a hand "dead"?

A dead hand means you cannot win that hand. Common causes:

  • Exposing a set that doesn't fit any valid NMJL hand pattern.
  • Claiming a tile and then discovering your remaining tiles can't complete any hand.
  • Declaring Mahjong with an invalid hand (you pay a penalty instead).

You continue playing (drawing and discarding to help others read your tiles), but you cannot declare Mahjong.

American vs. Chinese Mahjong

How is American Mahjong different from Chinese/Japanese Mahjong?

Key differences:

Can I make a sequence (Chow) like in Chinese Mahjong?

In American NMJL Mahjong, you do not call for a Chow (sequential run from another player's discard). Sequences still appear as part of specific winning hand patterns on the card, but you must build them entirely from your own drawn tiles — you cannot claim a discard to extend a sequence.

About This Game

How smart is the AI?

The AI uses a heuristic scoring approach: it prefers to keep tiles that form pairs and groups, discards isolated tiles first, and always calls Mahjong when it can win. It won't always play optimally — it won't read your hand or bluff — but it makes reasonable decisions. AI opponents:

  • Automatically complete the Charleston
  • Discard isolated honor tiles early
  • Keep Jokers and high-value sets
  • Call for Pung/Kong when it helps them win
Are the winning hands in this game from the official 2024 card?

The hands in this game are representative patterns based on common NMJL categories. They do not exactly reproduce any specific year's card (which is copyrighted by the NMJL). For official competitive play, always use the current NMJL card available from nationalmahjonggleague.org.

Can multiple humans play?

This game supports hot-seat play: by default you play as South against 3 AI opponents. To play multiple humans, replace the AI players in js/game-logic.js by making their seats human-controlled (the architecture fully supports it — the MJ.HUMAN_SEAT constant and event system are designed to be extensible).
